Case study

electromarjal.com: one platform, three languages, no framework

The commercial site for Marjal Instalaciones Eléctricas — built for a region where the customer might be Spanish, English or French, and the connection might be poor.

Role
Founder and sole developer
Stack
HTML5 · CSS3 · vanilla JS · JSON-LD · Docker · Nginx
Audience
Spanish residents, English expatriates, French retirees
Status
In service
1.0

A region with three customer bases

The Maestrat comarca — Peñíscola, Benicarló, Vinaròs — is unusually multi-demographic for its size. Residential work comes from Spanish locals, foreign property investors buying second homes, and French-speaking retirees. A single-language site loses two thirds of the enquiries before the first call.

The site also had to carry the transition from autónomo to S.L.: a premium physical service needed a digital presence that matched it, with official certifications and fixed pricing visible up front, because trust in this trade is established before anyone picks up a phone.

Remark

Spanish electrical documentation is governed by REBT. Showing the CIE certification prominently is not decoration — it is the fastest available proof that the installer can legally sign off the work.

2.0

Static routing beats a framework here

Mobile-first users in low-connectivity areas do not benefit from a client-side router. Languages are served as static subdirectories — /en/index.html, /fr/index.html — which gives crawlers a clean index per language and gives the visitor a document that renders before any script runs.

For local search visibility the pages carry Schema.org Electrician metadata in JSON-LD, declaring the physical address, geo-coordinates, hours and area served, so search engines know precisely where the service operates rather than inferring it.

index.html — structured data
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Electrician",
  "name": "Marjal Instalaciones Electricas",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "addressLocality": "Peniscola",
    "addressRegion": "Castellon",
    "postalCode": "12598",
    "addressCountry": "ES"
  },
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": 40.3582,
    "longitude": 0.4069
  }
}
Remark

No build step, no hydration, no bundle. The whole site is documents and a stylesheet, served by Nginx from a container.

3.0

The estimator answers the only question that matters

Solar enquiries stall on one question: what will I actually save. Answering it normally requires a site visit and a callback, which is where most leads are lost.

So the site estimates it in the browser. The visitor gives their average monthly bill and roof type; the calculator applies peak-sun-hour and system-loss coefficients for the Castellón coast and returns a system size and amortisation figure immediately, then serialises the inputs into a formatted enquiry. No database, no round trip, no account.

4.0

Why this sits in a machine learning portfolio

Because it is the business that funds the research, and because it is where the data comes from. Every installation and audit carried out under this company is a potential field capture for PanelSafe. The commercial work and the computer vision work are the same operation viewed from two ends.
