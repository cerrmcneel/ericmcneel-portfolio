1. The Real-World Bottleneck

Every electrical certificate (Boletín Eléctrico / CIE) and Technical Design Memorandum (MTD) submitted to regional industry authorities in Spain (such as the Conselleria d'Economia Sostenible in Comunitat Valenciana) legally requires an accurate Single-Line Electrical Diagram (Esquema Unifilar) conforming strictly to UNE-EN 60617 and ITC-BT-25 standards.

For certified installers, creating this diagram is a tedious bottleneck. After auditing a distribution board on-site, the electrician must return to the office, fire up generic CAD software, and spend 30 to 45 minutes manually dragging symbols, labelling wire gauges, and calculating short-circuit parameters. Generic CAD tools are cumbersome, prone to typographical errors, and disconnected from the physical reality of the breaker board.

2. The Solution: Downstream Visual Translation Engine

PanelSafe Unifilar bridges the gap between raw computer vision outputs and official regulatory blueprints. It takes the structured sequence extracted by the YOLO + HMM/Viterbi rail decoder and procedurally compiles it into a vector SVG diagram and technical load table in under 500 milliseconds.

3. Architectural Highlights

  • Procedural UNE-EN 60617 Symbol Engine: Pure mathematical SVG construction of standard symbols for Main Switches (IGA), Surge Protectors (PCS/SPD), Differential RCDs (ID), and Branch Circuit Breakers (PIAs) with dynamic multi-pole rendering (2P, 4P).
  • High-Speed Shorthand DSL: Electricians can tweak configurations on the fly using an intuitive domain-specific notation with instant live re-rendering:
PanelSafe Shorthand DSL Example Plaintext 
// PanelSafe Shorthand Code - Esquema Unifilar
[IGA:40A:2P] -> [SPD] -> [RCD:40A:30mA:2P]

// Standard branch circuits (REBT ITC-BT-25)
[PIA:10A:1.5mm:T18] -> C1 (Alumbrado)
[PIA:16A:2.5mm:T20] -> C2 (Tomas Generales)
[PIA:25A:6mm:T25]   -> C3 (Cocina y Horno)
[PIA:20A:4mm:T20]   -> C4 (Lavadora / Lavavajillas)
[PIA:16A:2.5mm:T20] -> C5 (Tomas Baño y Cocina)
[PIA:16A:2.5mm:T25] -> C13 (Vehículo Eléctrico)
  • Dynamic Technical Load Schedule: Automatically computes calculated power ($P_{cal}$), circuit voltage, minimum wire cross-sections ($S_f$ in mm² Cu), conduit diameter ($\varnothing$ in mm), and nominal circuit lengths according to Spanish REBT tables.
  • Regional Regulatory Metadata: Configurable compliance chips and headers tailored to regional industry registries (Comunitat Valenciana, Cataluña, Andalucía, Madrid) with direct high-resolution vector PDF and PNG exports.

4. Measurable Engineering Impact

By shifting schematic generation entirely to client-side vector rendering, PanelSafe Unifilar eliminates the need for expensive CAD licenses and heavy backend rendering servers. Installers can generate, inspect, and export submission-ready PDF schematics directly from their tablet or mobile device while standing in front of the customer's electrical board.
